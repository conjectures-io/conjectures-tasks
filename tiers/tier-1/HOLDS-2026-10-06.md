## How a hold is enforced

- `held-source-theorems.json` lists each held theorem with its previously published canonical type
  and its type at the current source. Selection refuses a held theorem by name or by any recorded
  type, exactly as it refuses a retired one. The tier policy commits to the file's digest in
  `held_source_theorems_sha256`.
- A held theorem is never also in `retired-source-theorems.json`. The two lists are disjoint by
  check, so a hold cannot quietly become a retirement or the reverse.
- The bundles are removed from the pool and the allowlist, so submissions against the old tasks
  fail closed with `TaskNotAllowed`.
- The old tasks stay readable through `retired-conjectures.json` with `pool_status: "held"` and the
  reason code above. The public API reports such a target as closed to submission with a withdrawn
  bounty, keeps its upstream `is_open` category, and never presents it as solved.

Re-admission removes the entry from `held-source-theorems.json` and is recorded in
`REINSTATEMENTS.md`, the same as any reinstatement.

The withdrawn task identities (bundle digests, task IDs, and types) of each target are in the
version 1 audit record; the release harness checks them against the deployed allowlist before any
file changes.
