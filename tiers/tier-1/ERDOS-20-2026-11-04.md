102, 103, 104, 111. Each number contributes one audited target and both
proof/counterexample modes. The pool retains 289 existing targets and adds 20.

Erdős 12 is presented with only part (iii), reciprocal summability, as its
remaining reward target. Parts (i) and (ii) are solved (yes and no). The Libertas claim is rejected because its `growth_ineq` is an
extra axiom and is false at index zero: for the standard good set of squares
of primes congruent to 3 modulo 4, the smallest element is 9, whereas the
axiom demands at least 30·3^8000. This does not decide the open conjecture.

REINSTATEMENTS.md records the new decision.

proof files and full review. The other nineteen targets use the bounded
October 28 intake review, with their upstream open labels refreshed November 4.
This is not an exhaustive literature or proof-claim search.

it contains more source files than this batch admits. Only the twenty selected
targets are added to the active allowlist. Historical bundle bytes and
verification admissions must remain intact.
